Night drive: the splash reveals, it does not zoom

The icon's scene stays exactly where it is and at the same size. The window around it opens outward and shows more of the map: the side streets, the turns in the route, the buildings, a park and a river.

Launcher icon

The lighter, bluer night map you picked. This is the base of the splash and is not changed by it.

What the reveal adds

The route turns out to come in from the left behind you and to turn right two blocks ahead. Side streets, city blocks, a park and a river fill in around it. The arrow, its beam and its brake lights never move or change size.

Four moments of the reveal

Notes

How it fits Android. When an app starts, Android itself shows the app icon in a circle in the middle of a plain background. The app controls what happens next, and that is where this reveal plays: the circle opens outward until the scene fills the screen, then the real map takes over.

Why the buildings fade in everywhere. The icon has no buildings, so that it stays clean at small sizes. During the reveal they fade in across the whole scene, including the corners inside the icon, so the finished picture has no bare patch in the middle.
