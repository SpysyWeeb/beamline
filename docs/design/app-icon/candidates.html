App icon, three ways

All three are built on the driving arrow from the app itself, with its brake lights, so the icon and the thing you watch while driving are the same object. No lettering, since the name is not settled.

Side by side at launcher size

Which I would pick

Blue field for the launcher. It reads most clearly at the smallest size, it stands out on both light and dark wallpapers, and it is the simplest shape to recognise at a glance.

Night drive is the most "ours". The beam and the red lights are the app's signature. It would make a good splash screen or store picture, and a fair launcher icon if you prefer a dark one.

Lanes tells the best story and reads the worst. It promises the lane-level map, which does not exist yet, and its details blur when small.
