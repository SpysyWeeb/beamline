How each part behaves

Puck
The route blue with a white ring, so it still reads when it sits on the route line.
Cone
Points where the phone's compass points and fades out with distance.
Arrow
Points along the direction of travel. Slightly raised: two facets meet at a centre ridge, with a thin rear face. In the tilted driving view it lies flat on the road.
Headlight
A soft beam on the road ahead, starting at the tip of the arrow, shown with the night map. There is no visible lamp.
Brake lights
Two small lenses set into the rear face, dark when off and lit red while the car is stopped.

Decided

  • Brake lights. Lit only while the car is stopped, and off as soon as it moves again.
  • Headlight. Shown with the night map, never on the day map.
  • Arrow shade. Stays in the same blue family as the route line, as drawn here.
  • Tilted view. Starts when guidance starts. Browsing stays top-down, even while driving.
