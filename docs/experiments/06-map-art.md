# Experiment 06: map artwork (icons, route shields, position markers, route colours)

Date: 2026-11-15. Machine: Windows 11, 12 logical CPUs, 32 GB RAM. **Other
engineers' jobs (two other tracks and three pack-builder fix passes) were
running during every measurement**, so times are pessimistic and noisy. Nothing
here used more than one CPU-heavy process at a time; the heaviest step was
headless Edge at about 1.1 GiB. During the review round (section 14) four
Android emulators and three Java processes of other engineers were running
(about 3 GB of RAM free), which is why the same steps took longer then.

This report was written by the first engineer of the track and then updated
after two independent reviews: **section 14 lists every review finding and what
happened to it**; sections 1 to 13 describe the track as it now stands.

## Result in short

- **The vector source of the bundled icons is published**: `sprites/refill.svg`
  in protomaps/basemaps (the Protomaps v4 sheets are generated from it by
  `sprites/src/main.rs`, which recolours grey templates per category). It is
  pinned (commit `42ffaaa4`, the same commit as our tile profile) and fetched by
  `tools/fetch_art_sources.py`. 89 icons and 10 shields are cut out of it into
  one SVG each under `assets/`, with the MIT notice of tangrams/icons.
  **Rebuilt with our pipeline, the 41 bundled icons that keep their size are
  pixel-identical to the bundled v4 sheet at 2x** (41 of 41) and at 1x (40
  pixel-identical, one, `capital`, with at most 6/255 difference). Old and new
  icons share the exact frame.
- **Sprite pipeline**: SVG templates in `assets/icons/`, `assets/shields/` →
  recolour per category and flavor → **spreet 0.14.0** (MIT, Windows binary
  used; the Linux build is pinned too but not run, SHA-256 pinned) packs 1x/2x
  sheets → `generate_style.mjs` copies them into the style bundle and checks
  that every icon the styles can ask for exists. One command: `build_art.py`,
  which checks its inputs first.
- **Icons**: 96 in the sheet (was 38 icons plus 15 shields). **7 drawn new**
  (parking, hospital "H", pharmacy, bank, ATM, truck stop, welcome center); the
  rest of the driving list comes from the upstream source (fuel, EV charging,
  parking garage, rest area, service plaza, toll booth, hotel, car repair, car
  wash, police, campground, viewpoint, car dealer ...). **All 100 search
  categories have an icon** (`check_poi_icons.py`), mapped together with 29 map
  POI kinds in `assets/poi_icons.json`. Truck stops on the map are found by
  name (the tiles have no brand tag): the rule now agrees with search's
  `truck_stop` category on 73 of its 88 PA places (was 15).
- **Shields**: US route (MUTCD M1-4 outline) and Pennsylvania keystone (M1-5),
  drawn by a script from the dimension sheets of PennDOT Publication 236, five
  widths each; wired by network (`US:I`, `US:US` and its banners, `US:PA` and
  its banners, fallback for the rest), numerals at contrast 8.9 to 17.4:1.
- **Markers**: 11 vector pieces from the approved sketch (puck, cone, arrow,
  beam, brake glow, lit lenses, stale puck and arrow), exported for Android at
  mdpi to xxxhdpi (0.62 MB in all), spec in `assets/markers/SPEC.md`. Checked
  on the map, including lying flat in the tilted view.
- **Route colours**: `assets/route_colors.json` with three candidates
  (A Sky = the sketch's blue, B Clear, C Periwinkle), alternative grey, driven
  part, widths by zoom; each rendered over downtown Erie and I-90 exit 24, day
  and night, with the arrow on the line. All are at least 23 (CIEDE2000) from
  the day water and at least 26 from every road fill and casing colour of the
  map. Lightest to darkest by day: A, C, B (the recommended B is the strongest
  line, not the lightest).
- **Style tuning** (measured with `count_pois.py`, z11 to z17, six views):
  rest areas and service plazas show from **z12-13** on the highway, parking
  from z13-14 in town, fuel and EV charging from **z15** (one z14 case in
  Pittsburgh); shops and cafes not before **z16** (supermarkets used to show at
  z14). Night street labels 9.85:1 and 7.95:1, enforced at 7:1; every POI label
  colour, the stock ones included, now at least 4.5:1 against its halo.
  Style bundle: 1,656,007 bytes, 781 files, 73 layers per style; validator and
  Native-support check pass (highest need: MapLibre Native Android 10.3.0; the
  app pins 13.7.1).
- **Contact sheet**: `assets/sheet/index.html` (2.99 MB, self-contained), PNG
  renders `data/art/sheet/sheet-01..09.png`, cut only in the gaps between
  blocks. I looked at every tile.

## 1. Sprite pipeline

### Where the bundled icons come from

`protomaps/basemaps-assets` (pinned `028c18f`) only holds finished sheets; its
README says they are "generated by spreet" and "derived from MIT-licensed
tangrams/icons". The generator and the source are in `protomaps/basemaps`
under `sprites/` (checked through the GitHub API at commit `42ffaaa4`):
`refill.svg` (810,484 bytes, 582 icon groups named `_<icon>`), `flavors/*.json`
(colour pairs per category) and `src/main.rs`. History of `refill.svg`: 4
commits, last "Shields (#518)", 2025-10-24. Licence: the repository's
`LICENSE.md` (BSD-3-Clause code, CC0-1.0 visual design, "Certain map styles
may also include derivatives of icons designed for Mapzen and released under
an MIT license"); tangrams/icons `LICENSE.md` is MIT, Copyright (c) 2017
Mapzen.

`main.rs` does three things that matter: drop the helper labels; use the union
of the shapes' bounding boxes plus 0.5 as the viewBox; and recolour every
`#rrggbb` channel `c` to `mix(pair[0], pair[1], c / 255)`. `refill.svg` draws
in greys (outline `#3f3f3f`, frame fill `#f4f4f4`, symbol fills `#d3d3d3`), so
one source gives every colour and theme.

### What we do

| Step | Script | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| fetch sources | `tools/fetch_art_sources.py` | refill.svg, flavors, main.rs, licences, and the v4 sheets for comparison, all SHA-256 pinned, into `data/art/upstream/` |
| fetch tools | `tools/fetch_art_tools.py` | spreet 0.14.0 (2.04 MB zip) and resvg 0.49.0 (1.76 MB zip) into `tools/bin/`; digests are GitHub's release digests, re-checked |
| import | `import_upstream_icons.py` | each group `_<id>` listed in `icons.json`/`shields.json` → one standalone SVG; CSS classes resolved to attributes; bounding box from `resvg --query-all` (leaf shapes, fill geometry) ± 0.5. Re-running writes nothing (`--check` confirms) |
| shields | `draw_shields.py` | US and PA shapes from dimensions (section 3) |
| sprites | `build_sprites.py` | recolour with the pair of the icon's group per flavor, file names `:`→`_`, spreet `--unique --minify-index-file` at ratio 1 and 2, index keys renamed back (`US:I-2char`) |
| style | `generate_style.mjs` | copies the sheets into the bundle |

Tool choice: spreet is what Protomaps' generator is built on (as a library),
and it ships a Windows binary; it rasterises with resvg and packs. resvg's CLI
renders the marker PNGs and answers the bounding-box queries. Both are
build-time only (MIT; Apache-2.0 OR MIT). Pillow does compositing and checks.

### Check against the bundled sheet (`build_sprites.py --compare-v4`)

| Sheet | same size as v4 | pixel-identical | not in ours |
|---|---|---|---|
| light, dark (1x) | 41 of 53 | 40 pixel-identical, 1 (`capital`) within 6/255 | 5 × NL:S-road |
| light@2x, dark@2x | 41 of 53 | 41 of 41 | 5 × NL:S-road |

"Pixel-identical" means every RGBA channel of every pixel is equal. The tool
prints the exact count and, separately, the images that differ by 1 to 8/255
with their largest difference (it used to print only one count "within 8/255").

The 7 others are shields: same shapes (compared at 4x), but the v4 images are
1 to 4 px wider (generic 1-char also 1 px taller). The difference is
transparent padding: the generator that made v4 (spreet 0.8 era) evidently
measured those stroked outlines with their stroke. Dutch `NL:S-road` shields
were dropped (US only).

| New icon | Symbol | Colour group |
|---|---|---|

| hospital | H, as on US hospital guide signs (MUTCD D9-2), not a cross | health (new, red) |
| pharmacy | capsule | health |
| bank | $ | blue |
| atm | machine with screen and note slot | blue |
| truck_stop | semi truck | auto (new, teal) |
| welcome_center | house with "i" | road (new, brown) |

New colour groups (pair = outline/symbol colour, pale fill; dark flavor on
`#1F1F1F` like the stock dark set): auto (fuel, EV, car services), road (rest
area, service plaza, welcome center, toll booth, RV dump), health, lodging
(hotel and motel), parking. Bundled flavors are unchanged, so the 41 bundled
icons look as before.

Mapping file `assets/poi_icons.json`:
- `categories`: all 100 ids of `packbuilder/search/categories.json` → 78
  distinct icons (food sub-categories reuse restaurant, fast food, cafe, bakery,
  deli, taqueria, seafood; motel, hotel and lodging share the bed).
- `map_kinds`: 29 tile `kind` values drawn by the new driving layer.
- `truck_stop_names`: a `fuel` **or `services`** POI gets the truck icon when
  its lower-cased name equals one of `starts_with` or begins with it and a space
  (`ta`, `ta express`, `pilot`, `flying j`, `love's`, `loves`, `petro stopping`,
  `one9`, `sapp bros`, `sapp brothers`, `bosselman`, `travelcenters of america`,
  `travel centers of america`), or contains one of `contains` (`travel center`,
  `travel centre`, `truck stop`, `truckstop`, `truck plaza`, `travel stop`,
  `truck fuel`). The word boundary keeps "Taco Bell" and "Petro Plus" out.
  `generate_style.mjs` turns the same list into a MapLibre expression
  (`downcase`, `index-of`, `in`).

  Checked against real names (`check_truck_stops.py`, all 3,823 PA POIs the
  search index files under fuel, service plaza or truck stop, by display name):

  | | truck stop in search and on the map | search only | map only |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | before the review (fuel only, 9 case-sensitive substrings) | 15 of 88 | 73 | 1 |
  | now | **73 of 88** | 15 | 6 |

  The 6 map-only places are named truck stops that search misses because they
  have no brand tag ("Pilot", "US Truck Stop", "Love's Travel Stop", "TA
  Express", "ONE9", "Bandit Truck Stop"). The 15 search-only ones are truck
  stops only by tags the tiles do not carry (HGV diesel, hgv=designated):
  "Onvo Travel Plaza" ×5, "Sheetz" ×3, "Sunoco" ×3, "Speedway", "Rutter's",
  "Pacific Pride", "Hickory Run Service Plaza". **Map and search can therefore
  still differ for those**; that needs a brand or hgv attribute in the tiles
  (map track). The style's expression and the Python rule give the same answer
  for all 608 distinct names × 3 kinds (1,824 cases, evaluated with the style
  spec's own expression evaluator). On the map: `check-truckstop-pilot-exit27-day.png`
  (two fuel POIs named "Pilot" and the "Pilot Travel Center" services POI all
  show the truck), `check-truckstop-ta-harborcreek-night.png` ("TA" fuel and
  services show the truck, "BP" the pump).

Real-size check: every icon on the day and night map as MapLibre draws it from
the sheet (`data/art/views/board-icons-*.png`, rendered at 2x = a phone's
density): all read at 19 dp, none blank or clipped (looked at full size).

PA 531, PA 832, PA 955, PA 5 Alternate, GLCT "LECT" (Lake Erie Circle Tour).

### Drawing

`draw_shields.py` writes 10 SVGs, 21 px tall at 1x like the bundled
Interstate:
- US route: outline from sheet M1-4 of PennDOT Publication 236 (March 2026):
  top peaks 5.0 in from the edges of a 24 in sign, scallops of 5.4 in radius,
  45-degree shoulders, waist, lower bulge, bottom point; the 30 in sheet widens
  the top and bottom for 3 digits.
- Pennsylvania: sheet M1-5: keystone top 1.0 in below the sign edge, top
  corners 5.0 in in, shoulders 5.0 in down, bottom corners 3.6 in in, sides at
  80.5 degrees. Both geometry sheets were read from the PDF's vector drawing
  (rendered for inspection with a throw-away converter, not kept).
- Widths 1-5 characters: US 19.5, 21, 28.2, 38.1, 45 px; PA 19.9, 21.7, 27.2,
  39, 46.3 px. The 4- and 5-character ones were widened after the shield board
  showed "322A" and "LECT1" touching the edges.
- Map shields are the shape with numerals only (white, thin dark outline;
  night: light grey `#d2d2d2`), as MUTCD 2D.11 paragraph 13 asks for route
  signs inside guide signs.

Style: `icon-image` chosen by network (`assets/shields/shields.json`), numeral
colour by network, `icon-size` 1 and `text-size` 10 (stock 0.8 and 8: same
ratio, 25 % larger for reading while driving). Shields seen on the map near
Erie and in Pittsburgh (US 19, US 20, PA 8, PA 28, PA 51, PA 97, PA 837, I-79,
I-90, I-279, I-376): `data/art/views/check-*-shields-*.png`. Ohio, from the
OH pack near Conneaut (`check-shields-ohio-conneaut-day.png`): OH 7 gets the
generic fallback shield, US 20 the US shield, I-90 the Interstate, as intended.

### Basis (primary sources)

- MUTCD 11th Edition (December 2023), Section 1D.06, paragraph 01 (Part 1 PDF,
  page 33): devices in the Manual "shall not be protected by a patent,
  trademark, or copyright, except for the Interstate Shield, 511 Travel
  Information pictograph, National Scenic Byway graphic, and any items under
  the stewardship of or owned by FHWA." The 2009 edition (Introduction,
  paragraph 04) excepted only the Interstate Shield and FHWA-owned items.
- MUTCD 11th Edition, Section 2D.11 (Chapter 2D PDF): paragraph 08 defines the
  U.S. Route sign (in the Manual); paragraph 10: "State Route signs shall be
  designed by the individual State highway agencies."
- Pennsylvania adopts the MUTCD "in its totality" (67 Pa. Code § 212.2(a),
  read on law.cornell.edu). PennDOT Publication 236 (March 2026, 839 pages,
  58 MB, downloaded once for reading, not kept in the repo) defines M1-5.
- **Could not confirm**: whether PennDOT claims any right in the keystone
  route marker. The text of all 839 pages of Publication 236 contains no
  "copyright", "trademark", "public domain" or "proprietary" (searched with
  pypdf; text only, not images). Trademark registers were not searched. The
  keystone is a plain outline of straight lines, drawn here from public
  dimensions.
- **Interstate**: kept as the bundled, monochrome, tangrams-derived shape. The
  MUTCD names the Interstate Shield as protected; whether a stylised map label
  needs permission was not settled. OpenStreetMap Americana was not consulted.

## 6. Style tuning

- **Driving POIs** (`pois_driving`, replaces the two dot-and-label layers): 29
  kinds with icons and coloured names, above the stock POI layer so they win
  collisions. `early_kinds` (fuel, charging station, parking, rest area,
  services) show one zoom before their `min_zoom`. The Protomaps profile puts
  named fuel stations into z15 tiles only (`min_zoom` 16), so **z15 is the
  earliest for named fuel and charging** without a profile change. Rest areas
  and service plazas (`min_zoom` 13-14, present in z12 tiles) and parking
  (`min_zoom` 12-15) are not limited that way.
- **Shops and cafes held back**: the stock `pois` layer now shows supermarket,
  convenience, books, beauty, electronics, clothes and cafe not before z16
  (`style_config.json` `stock_poi_min_zoom`), so that they come after fuel and
  charging. Before, supermarkets (`min_zoom` 14) appeared at z14.

  `queryRenderedFeatures` on a 412 × 800 view, day style, z11 to z17; number

  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

  | Erie downtown | - | z14 (13) | - | - | z16 (1) | **none** (was z15 (2)) |
  | Pittsburgh downtown | z14 (1) | z13 (2) | - | z16 (1) | z16 (14) | - |
  | I-79 rest area, McKean | - | - | z13 (1) | - | - | - |
  | Turnpike, Sideling Hill Service Plaza | z15 (1) | - | z12 (1) | z16 (1) | z16 (2) | - |

  (they lose collisions to the driving layer). No group was placed at z11 in
  any of the six views. Only the shop counts changed with the fix (5 of 252
  counts); everything else is identical before and after.
- **Label contrast** (text against its halo, WCAG): night street labels 9.85:1
  (major) and 7.95:1 (minor), the generator stops below 7:1 for them; driving

## 9. Failures, dead ends, not verified

- **Not run on MapLibre Native Android**: the new sprites, style and markers
  were only rendered in MapLibre GL JS 6.16 in headless Edge. The static
  support check passes, and the properties used by the marker layers exist on
  Android since 5.2.0.
- The style spec has only 1x and 2x sprite sheets; on 3x phones MapLibre
  scales the 2x sheet up (slightly soft icons). Not measured on a device.
- The upstream Interstate shield is too narrow for 4-5 characters (PA has only
  2-3 digit Interstates).
- Truck stops: map and search still disagree for 21 of the 3,823 PA fuel and
  service places (section 2), because the tiles carry no brand or hgv tag.
- One render run hit `ERR_CONNECTION_REFUSED` on a glyph request (local
  server listen backlog of 5 under load); fixed by a backlog of 128 in
  `render_art.py`, then 25 of 25 views clean (28 of 28 in the review run).
- PA keystone rights: not confirmed either way (section 3).
- Ohio: one view from the OH pack (shields near Conneaut); nothing else of the
  art was checked on Ohio data.
- **Linux**: `tools/fetch_art_tools.py` pins the Linux x86_64 builds of spreet
  and resvg (GitHub release digests; downloaded and SHA-256-checked here, both
  are ELF binaries), and the scripts pick `tools/bin/<tool>/<tool>` and
  `.venv/routing/bin/python` there; `render_art.py --channel chromium` would
  use Playwright's own Chromium instead of Edge. **None of this was run on
  Linux** (WSL is kept for tools that have no Windows build).
- `fetch_style_inputs.py` still downloads the v4 sheets into
  `data/map/style-src/` (now unused by the generator); left as it is.

## 12. Decisions for the owner

1. **Route blue**: A, B or C (sheet section 4). My recommendation is **B**:
   strongest edge on the day map (casing 4.71:1) and furthest from water by
   day, still clear at night (4.34:1). Be aware that B is the **darkest** of
   the three (L* 58.9 by day against 68.2 for A and 66.7 for C): if "light blue,
   easy on the eyes" matters more than edge strength, A (the sketch's own
   colour, the lightest by day) or C (light by day, brightest at night) fit
   that brief better. Until then the app should use `default_until_selected` (A).
2. **Approve the 7 new icons and the US and PA shields** (sheet sections 1-2),
   and the shield size (text 10, 25 % larger than stock).
3. **Interstate shield**: keep the bundled monochrome shape, recolour it
   red/white/blue, or replace it (it is the one design the MUTCD calls
   protected).
4. **Keystone rights**: have the PA keystone checked before a public release
   (no statement found either way).
5. **Marker exports in the app**: commit the PNGs into the Android resources,
   or build them as part of the app build (the Android track's call).
6. **When driving POIs appear**: fuel and charging from z15 is the limit of the
   current tiles; earlier needs the map track's profile (named fuel and
   charging at min zoom 13-14). Parking shows from z14 in town (13 icons
   downtown Erie at z14): keep, or hold parking back to z15 with one number in
   `poi_icons.json`. Recommendation: keep both as they are until the owner has
   driven with it.
