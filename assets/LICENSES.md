## Route shields

- **Federal basis.** MUTCD 11th Edition (December 2023), Section 1D.06
  "Public Domain, Copyrights, and Patents", Standard, paragraph 01: "Traffic
  control device design or application provisions contained in this Manual
  shall be in the public domain. Traffic control devices contained in this
  Manual shall not be protected by a patent, trademark, or copyright, except
  for the Interstate Shield, 511 Travel Information pictograph, National
  Scenic Byway graphic, and any items under the stewardship of or owned by
  FHWA." (mutcd.fhwa.dot.gov/pdfs/11th_Edition/part1.pdf, page 33.) The 2009
  edition said the same in its Introduction, paragraph 04, with only the
  Interstate Shield and FHWA-owned items excepted.
- **US route shield** (M1-4) is a device contained in the Manual: Section
  2D.11, paragraph 08 ("U.S. Route signs (see Figure 2D-4) shall consist of
  black numerals on a white shield surrounded by a rectangular black
  background without a border"), Chapter 2D of the 11th Edition. Drawn here
  from the dimensions of sheet M1-4 in PennDOT Publication 236, Handbook of
  Approved Signs, March 2026 edition. Not the Interstate shield.
- Section 2D.11, paragraph 13 says that where route signs are used inside
  guide signs "only the distinctive shape of the shield itself and the route
  numerals within should be used", without the black rectangle. The map
  shields follow that: shape and numerals only.
- **Pennsylvania route keystone** (M1-5): a State design; MUTCD 2D.11
  paragraph 10: "State Route signs shall be designed by the individual State
  highway agencies." Drawn here from
  sheet M1-5 of PennDOT Publication 236 (March 2026): a keystone of straight
  sides at 80.5 degrees, white with a black border and numerals.
  Pennsylvania adopts the MUTCD "in its totality" (67 Pa. Code § 212.2(a)).
  Basis for use: it is a plain geometric outline of a public traffic sign,
  drawn from public dimensions. **Not confirmed:** I found no statement by
  PennDOT on copyright or trademark of the M1-5 design, either way, and did not
  search trademark registers. The owner may want that checked before release.
- **Interstate shield**: the bundled Protomaps shape (tangrams-derived, MIT),
  kept monochrome. The MUTCD names the Interstate Shield as the one protected
  design; whether a stylised map label of that shape needs permission was not
  settled here. It is an owner decision whether to keep it, recolour it in the
  red/white/blue of the real sign, or replace it with a neutral shape.
